How NexusProcure Works

From quote receipt to agreed terms, the AI works alongside your team to save money without damaging supplier relationships.

1

1. Set Target Budget

Enter the supplier's initial quote and define your target price and walk-away budget. You remain in complete control of the guardrails.

2

2. AI Evaluates & Counters

Powered by Groq LLM reasoning, the AI identifies trade-offs (like volume discounts or 10-day payment terms) and emails a polite, firm counter-offer.

3

3. 1-Click Approval

When the supplier accepts or compromises, review the final agreed price and approve with one click to generate the official Purchase Order.
