Game-Theoretic BATNA Solver

How the agent models bargaining curves and finds the Pareto optimal settlement zone.

Dynamic Concession Decay Function

Rather than making linear concessions, NexusProcure AI uses a non-linear concession function that incorporates time decay, supplier switching costs, and current market index volatility.

P(t) = P_target + (P_quote - P_target) * e^(-k * (t / t_deadline)) * (1 - α * S_alternative)
where: k = urgency factor, α = alternative supplier leverage weight, S_alternative = normalized BATNA quote spread
Zero Anchoring Bias

Never falls prey to inflated opening supplier quotes. Always roots initial offers in market raw material index realities.

Multi-Issue Tradeoffs

If a vendor refuses a price discount, the agent trades payment terms (Net-30 to Net-15) or MOQ commitments for unit savings.

Hard Policy Ceilings

Guaranteed mathematical bounds. The model is hard-coded never to exceed your predefined max spend without cryptographic human escalation.
